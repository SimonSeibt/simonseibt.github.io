<p style="text-align: justify;">On July 17, 2025, the Industry 4.0 networking event on virtual commissioning took place at Allgäu Digital in Kempten. The event explored practical experiences, success stories and lessons learned in virtual commissioning, its economic viability and integration into company workflows, and offered perspectives on the Industrial Metaverse.</p>
